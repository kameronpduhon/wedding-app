# Wedding App — Color Palette Options

Palettes designed for a wedding vendor coordination app. Target user: brides planning their wedding. Should feel trustworthy, calming, and elegant.

---

## 🌸 Option 1: Soft Romance

Warm, blush-forward palette. Classic bridal feel.

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Primary | Dusty Rose | `#D4A5A5` |
| Secondary | Soft Blush | `#F5E6E0` |
| Accent | Rose Gold | `#B76E79` |
| Background | Warm White | `#FFFAF8` |
| Text | Charcoal | `#3D3D3D` |
| Muted | Warm Gray | `#9C9C9C` |

**Vibe:** Romantic, feminine, traditional. Appeals to classic/romantic wedding styles.

---

| Accent | Eucalyptus | `#5C7C65` |

---

## Recommendation

For a vendor coordination app, I'd lean toward **Option 2 (Modern Garden)** or **Option 3 (Elegant Luxe)**:

- **Modern Garden** feels fresh and organized — matches the "getting stuff done" energy of coordinating vendors
- **Elegant Luxe** feels premium and trustworthy — makes brides feel like they're using a high-quality tool
