# Wed & Gather — Logo Concepts

## Color Palette
- Primary: `#87A98F` (sage green)
- Dark: `#5C7C65` (deep sage)
- Accent: `#D4A5A5` (soft pink)
- Light: `#E8F0E9` (pale sage)

---

## Concept 1: Clean Wordmark

```
Wed & Gather
```
- Modern sans-serif font (like Inter, Outfit, or Poppins)
- The "&" slightly larger or in accent color
- Simple, professional, scales well

**Variations:**
- `Wed & Gather` — all same weight
- `Wed` **&** `Gather` — ampersand bold/colored
- `WED & GATHER` — all caps, more editorial

---

## Concept 2: Botanical Wordmark

```
  🌿
Wed & Gather
```
- Small leaf/branch element above or beside the name
- Or a subtle leaf integrated into a letter (like the "G")
- Feels organic, wedding-appropriate

---

## Concept 3: Ampersand Focus

Make the "&" the star:
- Stylized ampersand that looks like intertwined rings
- Or an ampersand with a small leaf accent
- Could be used as standalone icon

---

## Concept 4: Monogram Icon

```
W&G
```
- Compact monogram for favicon, app icon
- Could have a circle or soft square container
- The "&" centered, W and G on either side

---

## Concept 5: Ring Integration

- Two interlocking rings forming the "&" 
- Subtle, not too literal
- Works as icon and with wordmark

---

## Font Recommendations

**Primary (Wordmark):**
- **Outfit** — Modern, friendly, geometric
- **Poppins** — Rounded, approachable
- **Cormorant Garamond** — Elegant, wedding-y (serif option)

**For a softer feel:**
- **Playfair Display** — Classic, romantic serif
- **Lora** — Warm, readable serif

---

## My Recommendation

Start with **Concept 1 or 2** — a clean wordmark, possibly with a small botanical element. 

Why:
- Scales well (works tiny and large)
- Doesn't try too hard
- Feels professional but warm
- Easy to implement now, can refine later

The monogram (W&G) can be the favicon/app icon.
