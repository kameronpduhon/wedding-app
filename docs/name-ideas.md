# Wedding App — Name Ideas

Brainstorm session, Feb 2026.

---

## Top Picks

### **Aisle**
- Clean, one word, instantly wedding-related
- "Organize everything down the aisle"
- Domain: aisle.app, getaisle.com, aisleapp.com

### **Bower**
- A bower is a shelter/arbor often used in weddings
- Soft, elegant, memorable
- "Your wedding vendor bower"

### **Gather**
- Action word — you're gathering documents
- Warm, inviting
- Could be: GatherWedding, GatherApp

### **VendorVow**
- Alliteration, memorable
- Clear what it does (vendors + wedding vow)
- "Your vendors, your vow, all in one place"

## Recommendation

**"Aisle" or "Bower"** stand out as the strongest options:
- Short and memorable
- Instantly wedding-related
- Sound professional but warm
- Easy to build a brand around

Second tier: **Gather** or **VendorVow**.
